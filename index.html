FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Auto Mode?

Auto Mode is the default way to chat on Grizon AI. Each message is routed to the best eligible specialised agent for your plan — so you do not have to pick manually unless you want to.

How does the credit system work?

Each plan includes monthly credits. Agent responses consume credits based on usage (tokens and the agent's cost multiplier). You can see your balance in the app wallet and settings. Paid plans can top up credits when enabled.

Is my data secure on Grizon AI?

Yes. Conversations and files are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not use your chat data to train AI models.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings. There are no lock-in contracts.

What can the Video Analyser do?

On Pro and Elite, Video Analyser reviews uploaded video files — describing scenes, extracting on-screen text, identifying events, and answering questions about the footage.
